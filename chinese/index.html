    The four tones

    The neutral (5th) tone is short and light, like the second syllable of 妈妈 ma. Colors used throughout the app match these tones.

    Tone rules

    • 3 + 3 → 2 + 3: 你好 is said ní hǎo.
    • 不 bù becomes bú before a 4th tone: 不对 bú duì.
    • 一 yī becomes yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí gè) and yì before others (一点 yì diǎn).
