Pulse

A music visualizer that moves with your sound.

Which should I pick?

Microphone picks up whatever is playing out loud: your iPhone speaker, a Bluetooth speaker, a TV or a live band. Works with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and anything else.

Music files plays songs from the Files app (MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC…) and is analysed directly, so it's in perfect sync and works with headphones.

iOS doesn't let any app read audio from other apps directly, so streaming apps work through the microphone.

Tip: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to use Pulse full screen like a regular app.
